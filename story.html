The History of Redding

Chapter One

The Coming of the Moon

They will tell you Redding was always a quiet town, and they will be lying to keep you calm. The quiet came later, after the woods were cut back and the lamps were lit along the high road — a quiet bought dearly, and paid for still.

Before the lamps there was only the moon, and the moon has a long memory. On the nights it swelled full over the moor, the old families locked their doors and pretended not to hear what moved between the pines. They called them strays — the wanderers, the moon-touched — as though a gentler word might make them gentler things.

It did not. And this is where their history begins.
